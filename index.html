Research notes

  1. Controlled inhalation studies found orderly dose-related increases in overall drug-strength ratings, with effects peaking very rapidly.
  2. Reported domains include dissociation, altered spatial orientation, unusual body sensations, perceptual alteration and changes in cognition.
  3. Higher experimental exposure was associated in some participants with temporary unresponsiveness and greater recall / recognition memory impairment.
  4. Individual response varied substantially. Anxiety, distress or disorientation occurred for some people, while others described neutral, unusual or positively valenced experiences.
  5. A label such as 10x or 50x describes claimed concentration, not a distinct scientific experience category. No human trial establishes a unique “50x effect.”

Primary references

  1. MacLean et al. (2013), dose-related dissociative, hallucinogenic and memory effects
  2. Johnson et al. (2011), human psychopharmacology and dose-effects
  3. Ranganathan et al. (2012), behavioral and perceptual effects in humans

The calculator uses these studies for effect domains, not to convert retail extract labels into exact absorbed microgram doses.
